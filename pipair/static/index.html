      Library

      GPI triggers

      Wire a switch or relay contact between a GPIO pin and any GND pin (header pins 6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34 or 39). The Pi's internal pull-up holds the pin high; closing the contact pulls it low and counts as closes. For 3.3 V logic outputs, use pull-down and active high under Advanced. Never connect 5 V to a GPIO pin.
